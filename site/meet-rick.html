Richard “Rick” L. Senninger, CGC, CHt., is the creator of the Transforming Individual Performance (TIP) Hypnosis Method and a three-time national award-winning Family Service Counselor.

As a Clinical Hypnotherapist, Certified Grief Counselor, Mentor, Published Scholar, and Author of the acclaimed book 10,000 Funerals, Rick is widely recognized as one of the most forward-thinking practitioners in personal transformation and human behavior and the experiences of being human.

Rick’s clients consistently describe him as “spot on” — a rare professional who sees and hears each individual with remarkable clarity. His sessions are vivid, direct, deeply engaging, and purposeful. Known for his precision, Rick has an exceptional ability to translate complex patterns of human behavior into practical, immediate change — earning him a reputation as a master hypnotist and elite performance mentor.

Through TIP Hypnosis, Rick integrates advanced subconscious behavioral strategies with each client’s personal and professional goals, creating a highly personalized, private, one-on-one experience. His work is designed to produce dynamic, measurable, and sustainable results — whether addressing anxiety, grief, performance, or confidence.

As an independent researcher and published scholar, Rick’s work extends beyond the clinical setting. He explores the deeper dimensions of consciousness and human experience, integrating insights from neuroscience, psychology, philosophy, theology, and life-after-life studies — offering both transformative outcomes and meaningful perspective.
